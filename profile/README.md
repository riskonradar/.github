# risk on radar.

**Evidence-backed failure intelligence for reliability and quality engineering teams.**

Every FMEA starts from a blank row and expert memory. We index peer-reviewed failure literature into a structured knowledge layer — so analyses are grounded in global evidence, not just who's in the room.

## What we're building

Traditional FMEA is broken by design — teams copy old analyses, score risks from memory, and file documents that are never updated. We're fixing the knowledge layer.

| Phase | Status |
|---|---|
| **01 · Failure Intelligence Engine** — living knowledge graph from 2,800+ peer-reviewed papers, 75+ component types | **In progress** |
| **02 · System-Level Risk Analysis** — cross-system failure propagation and dependency mapping | Planned |
| **03 · Cross-Domain Failure Intelligence** — unified failure knowledge across automotive, aerospace, industrial, and energy | Planned |
